01 — The problem

The portal wasn't built for volume.

01

Keyed one at a time

Each instrument takes 5–10 minutes to enter by hand. A 200-instrument batch is a week of clerk time — every single month.

02

One typo, one rejection

A wrong IC format or a date typed as MM/DD instead of DD/MM bounces the submission — and under self-assessment, the liability sits with you.

03

Attachments, encoded by hand

The bulk spec wants every supporting PDF base64-encoded inside the XML. That is not a job for a human with a text editor.

05 — FAQ

Common questions.

What is the STAMPS Bulk Generator?

A Windows desktop application that converts Excel spreadsheets into LHDN STAMPS-compliant bulk stamping XML for e-Duti Setem (MyTax). It lets law firms, banks and corporate secretaries stamp many instruments in one upload instead of keying each one in by hand.

Does it work with LHDN e-Duti Setem (MyTax)?

Yes. The STAMPS portal moved to e-Duti Setem on MyTax in 2026, and the generator produces the bulk stamping XML format used for that system.

Can it generate XML for both Penyeteman Sekuriti and Penyeteman Am?

Yes. A single dropdown switches between Penyeteman Sekuriti (applicationType 43) and Penyeteman Am (applicationType 44); the template, validation and generated XML all adapt automatically.

How does it handle the 30 MB STAMPS upload limit?

When your output would exceed 30 MB, it automatically splits the data into multiple numbered files, each safely under the limit and ready to upload one by one — no manual splitting.

Does my data stay private and work offline?

Yes. It runs 100% offline on your computer. Your spreadsheets and attachments never leave your machine or get uploaded to any server.

How much does the STAMPS Bulk Generator cost?

An annual license of RM 490 per year — including both Sekuriti and Am modes, the Excel template, and all updates and support while your license is active.
